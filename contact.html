FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

Econo is ready in 14 working days, Premium in 21 and Prestige in 30 — counted from the day we receive all of your materials.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Every package includes our own VCMS plus physical or Zoom training, so your staff can update pages, news and galleries without touching code.

Do you handle the domain and hosting too?

Yes. We register .com, .biz, .net, .org and the full MYNIC range, and host on our own cPanel servers with scheduled backups and corporate email.

Can you move my existing website to VISHTECH?

Yes. We handle the full migration — files, databases, DNS and mailboxes. Contact our sales team and we'll scope it before you commit.

What happens after the free maintenance period?

You can continue on one of our Maintenance & Support packages, which cover monthly content updates, SEO monitoring and backups during office hours.
