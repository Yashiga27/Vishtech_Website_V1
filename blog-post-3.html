Our current web design, hosting and digital marketing offers, and what is included in each one.

Econo, Premium and Prestige

Our three web design packages start at MYR2,400 and run through to MYR8,800, with an unlimited-page Prestige tier for corporates and public listed holdings. Every tier includes our own CMS, a free .com domain, hosting, corporate email, an SSL certificate and 30 days of free maintenance.

Add a year of maintenance

Each package has a matching + 1 Year Maintenance version that bundles monthly content, banner, news and gallery updates with SEO monitoring and monthly backups. It works out cheaper than paying for the updates one at a time.

Domain and hosting bundles

Domains start from RM80/year across .com, .biz, .net, .org and the full MYNIC range, and hosting from RM150/year with cPanel, webmail, Softaculous and scheduled backups.

If your site is hosted elsewhere, we'll migrate the files, databases, DNS and mailboxes for you. Contact our sales team and we'll scope the move before you commit to anything.
